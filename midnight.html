How to escape

Walk with ↑↓ (or W/S), turn with ←→, and press SPACE at anything with a brass tag. Drag the mouse to look up or down.

Each tagged apparatus hides one digit of its corner’s cabinet code. Open the cabinet, solve the problem inside, and take the key. Three keys open the door.

Wrong answers cost 30 seconds. Hints cost 10 seconds: press H for directions, or use the hint button inside a puzzle. If the needle reaches midnight, the lab is lost and you start over.
